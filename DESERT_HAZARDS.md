# READY PLAYER ONE - DESERT WASTELAND RACE HAZARDS
# Dry / Sandy / Rocky - Stop / Slow / Destroy

> Design philosophy: Every hazard has a READ, a RISK, and a COUNTERPLAY.
> No generic sandstorms. Everything is physical, readable, and clip-worthy.

---

### I. SLOW DOWN - Attrition / Control Hazards
You survive, but you lose the lead.

**1. White-Out Alkali Pan**
A blinding white salt flat. Not water - fine alkaline powder. 
- Effect: Vision -80%, engine intake clogs (progressive speed loss 10% -> 40%), tire chemical burn.
- Tell: Blinding white, dead animal skeletons around edge.
- Counter: Stick to darker tracks left by previous racers, or activate air-filter purge.

**2. Magnetite Black Dune**
Sand that is 90% iron filings. Looks like black glitter.
- Effect: All metal vehicles get magnetic drag, steering pulls left/right, brakes overheat. Hover vehicles unaffected.
- Tell: Compass goes crazy, sand stands up in spikes.
- Counter: Non-metallic tires / power slide through it.

**3. The Glass Sea**
Old nuclear test melted the desert into a thin sheet of cracked glass.
- Effect: Ultra low friction + glass cracks under you. Slow vehicles crack it and fall through slightly (like thin ice). Fast vehicles can hydroplane over it.
- Tell: Reflective, you can see your own car, spiderweb cracks.
- Counter: Risk/reward - go FAST to skip over, or slow and break.

**4. Creosote Root Snare**
The surface looks clear, but ancient creosote roots form a net just under the sand.
- Effect: Random tire grab, car jerks and loses momentum. If you stop, roots wrap.
- Tell: Small identical bushes in perfect circles.
- Counter: Don't stop. Bunny-hop / boost over.

**5. Slickrock Soapstone**
Polished canyon floor worn smooth by wind for 10k years.
- Effect: Zero grip. You can't turn or brake, only slide.
- Tell: Shiny pink-orange rock, covered in old tire marks.
- Counter: Use walls to drift-bounce like a bobsled.

**6. Tar-Brea Bleed**
Natural tar seeping up, covered in a thin dust layer so it looks like normal sand.
- Effect: Tires get gummed, stacking slow debuff. Makes you sticky for other hazards.
- Tell: Slight rainbow sheen, flies stuck in it, bubbles popping.
- Counter: Drive through a dust cloud right after to "dry" your tires.

**7. Mirage Highway (OASIS Glitch)**
An OASIS render error - a perfect highway appears in the desert that isn't really there.
- Effect: You drive onto it and your wheels spin on air while your speedo says 300mph but you're actually sinking in sand.
- Tell: Too perfect, no dust, slight pixel flicker.
- Counter: Look at your shadow - if shadow is below the road, it's fake.

**8. Static Devil**
A 50ft dust devil that is electrically charged.
- Effect: Doesn't damage, but scrambles your HUD, mini-map, and reverses steering for 5 seconds.
- Tell: Crackles with blue lightning inside.
- Counter: Honk horn / EMP pulse to discharge it early.

### II. STOP - Full Immobilize Traps
You are dead-stopped. Need skill/item/teammate to get out.

**9. Antlion Pits**
100ft wide perfect cones in the sand. The classic antlion trap but car-sized.
- Effect: You slide to the bottom, walls are too steep to climb. Engine overheats trying.
- Tell: Perfect circle, sand flowing inward.
- Counter: Keep momentum on the rim, or use grapple/nitro to wall-ride out.

**10. Fossil Titan Ribcage**
A colossal dinosaur skeleton half-buried, forming a gate.
- Effect: You have to weave through ribs. If you hit a rib, it collapses like a cage around you.
- Tell: Giant bones casting long shadows.
- Counter: Shoot/honk to test which ribs are brittle before entering.

**11. The Slot Squeeze**
A slot canyon that dynamically narrows.
- Effect: Only one car fits. If two enter, both get wedged. Canyon walls scrape and lock you.
- Tell: Walls have paint from previous racers.
- Counter: Be first, or brake and let idiot in front get stuck as your bridge.

**12. Junkball - Tumble Titan**
A rolling ball of barbed wire, cars, fridges, and tumbleweeds 30ft across pushed by wind.
- Effect: Can't be destroyed, just rolls. If it hits you, you get tangled and dragged.
- Tell: You hear it rattling from a mile away.
- Counter: Shoot the core to change its direction into other players.

**13. Caltrop Cactus Mortars**
Cacti that evolved to shoot their spines.
- Effect: They fire 3ft hardened spines that are natural caltrops. Pop tires, jam treads.
- Tell: They puff up before firing, like they inhale.
- Counter: Drive in their shadow - they track the sun, not you.

**14. Mine Shaft Sinkhole**
Thin crust over an old mine shaft.
- Effect: Floor drops, front wheels hang over void. You teeter.
- Tell: Wooden support beams poking up, ground sounds hollow.
- Counter: Reverse immediately, don't gun it. Weight balance mini-game.

**15. Lasso-Bot Ghost Town**
Automated cowboy show robots still running.
- Effect: They throw energy lassos that catch roll-cages and yank you to a stop for 3 sec.
- Tell: You hear "YEEHAW!" and a twirling sound.
- Counter: Duck/crouch vehicle suspension to slip lasso.

**16. Vulture Latch Drones**
Solar-powered vulture drones that think dead cars are food.
- Effect: Land on you and weigh 2 tons, won't let go until you shake them.
- Tell: Shadows circling above.
- Counter: Drive under a rock overhang, they are solar and scared of dark.

### III. DESTROY - Lethal / Wrecking Hazards
Mistakes = explosion / total loss.

**17. Solar Kill Tower**
An abandoned solar farm. Thousands of mirrors all track one tower. The beam sweeps like a lighthouse.
- Effect: Focused sunlight = 3000 degrees. Melts tires, cooks engine in 2 seconds. Leaves a molten glass trail.
- Tell: Loud hum, all mirrors moving together, bright dot on ground.
- Counter: Watch the sweep pattern and drive in its shadow.

**18. Geode Shrapnel Field**
Beautiful purple quartz geodes sticking out of sand. Looks like loot.
- Effect: They are pressure mines. Drive over = crystal shrapnel shotgun upwards, shreds undercarriage.
- Tell: Too beautiful, too perfect, humming slightly.
- Counter: Shoot them from distance to detonate for other players.

**19. UXO Dunes - The Bombing Range**
WW2 / future war bombing range, half-buried bombs everywhere.
- Effect: Not all explode - some are duds that launch your car 50ft in the air when you hit them like a ramp, some are live.
- Tell: Fins sticking up, warning signs that say "DO NOT".
- Counter: Only drive on existing crater rims - safe path.

**20. Turbine Graveyard**
200 broken wind turbines, blades hanging by a wire.
- Effect: Wind gust triggers a blade to fall like a giant guillotine, slicing cars in half. Creates new obstacles.
- Tell: Creaking, blades swaying, shadows spinning even with no wind.
- Counter: Honk to trigger fall early, then pass.

**21. Flash Flood Wash**
Bone-dry riverbed. No water for 100 years... until it isn't.
- Effect: Distant thunder sound, then 10ft wall of water, logs, and rocks appears in 4 seconds. Washes everything sideways into rocks.
- Tell: The air goes cold, you smell rain, birds fly away.
- Counter: High ground. Always have an escape route up the wash bank.

**22. Razor Flats - Obsidian Field**
Canyon where walls are volcanic glass, wind-sharpened to razor.
- Effect: Get too close to wall and it shaves your car doors/tires off. Sparks everywhere.
- Tell: Black glass, incredibly sharp reflections, shredded tire piles at entrance.
- Counter: Center line only. Use it to shave OTHER players by PIT maneuvering them into wall.

**23. Geyser Timing Field**
Geothermal field disguised as normal rock.
- Effect: Superheated steam geysers on a 7-second timer. Direct hit = engine explosion, near miss = car launched.
- Tell: Sulfur smell, rock discoloration, ground trembles before.
- Counter: Learn the rhythm - it's musical. Count it.

**24. Meteor Paint Targeting**
Old orbital defense satellite still active, targets reflective objects.
- Effect: If your car is shiny/clean, it paints you with a red laser. 3 seconds later a small meteor / rod from god strikes that spot.
- Tell: Red dot appears on hood, beeping gets faster.
- Counter: Get dirty. Drive through tar or dust to become matte.

**25. Sand Compressed Air Cannons**
Natural rock formations that act like pressure cannons.
- Effect: Blast of sand at Mach 2. Strips all upgrades off car, sandblasts windshield opaque, stalls engine.
- Tell: Holes in rock whistling loudly.
- Counter: Cover intake, close shutters before passing.

**26. The Bone Worm (Not Dune, Worse)**
A skeletal snake made of fused animal bones and scrap, burrowing just under sand.
- Effect: Hunts vibration. If you go full throttle in open sand for >5 sec, it bursts up and bites, crushing car.
- Tell: Sand ripples moving against wind.
- Counter: Tap brake to go quiet, or make other players be louder.

**27. ACME Coyote Boxes**
Wile E. Coyote style trap boxes left by a Looney Tunes event.
- Effect: Says "FREE NITRO" but it's an anvil drop / spring-loaded boxing glove / dynamite.
- Tell: Too cartoony, slightly out of place, has that ACME logo.
- Counter: You know you shouldn't... but you will.

### BONUS: COMBO IDEAS (Ready Player One style)

- **Magnetite + Turbine Graveyard:** Magnet pulls falling blade toward you.
- **Glass Sea + Solar Tower:** Glass reflects solar beam in random directions.
- **Antlion Pit + Geyser:** Pit is actually a geyser mouth.
- **Flash Flood + Tar Pit:** Water pushes you INTO tar, double trap.

---

### Quick Reference for Devs
- Slow = -20-50% speed, recoverable
- Stop = 3-10 sec immobilized, needs action
- Destroy = 70-100% damage, high spectacle death

All hazards should have: Visual Tell (2 sec warning) -> Audio Tell (1 sec) -> Consequence
