How to Use

1 — Draw: Press 2, pick preset, click existing node or empty ground to create nodes & segments. Keep clicking to extend. Intersections auto-split.
2 — Edit: Press 1, drag nodes, click segment to change type in inspector. Del to delete. Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y undo.
3 — Terrain: Click ⛰, drag to raise, Shift+Drag to lower. Height drives road embankments & bridge triggers.
4 — 3D: Orbit/dolly in right viewport. Double-click road to focus. Use Overview/City/Countryside/Offroad buttons.
5 — Topology: Hit Clean Topology to re-merge intersections — handles same-type and mixed-type crossings (highway × street, dirt × rural, rail × road) with topology-safe caps & UV continuity.
6 — Export: Export JSON and re-import to share networks.
GTA-6 Grade: All roads build as contiguous quad strips with mitered joins, continuous UVs, curb bevels, bridge decks/piers/beams/railings, crosswalks, ramps. No low-poly — PBR materials, soft shadows, ACES tonemapping.
