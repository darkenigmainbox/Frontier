Frontier — Hybrid SDF Terrain Generator

  
What merged: the particle-branch's live GPU agents (rain / runoff / river / wind / rockfall / chemical) with the algorithmic branch's volumetric SDF authoring (CSG stamps, strata hardness, micro-detail) — plus fixes for both ancestors' failure modes.

  
No more runaway holes: erosion demand is strictly bounded by the particle's free sediment capacity (budget), worn rock accumulates armor (integral feedback), stalled water dumps its load into pits, and a thin-wall guard avoids puncturing fins and arches. Headless A/B: trapped particles converge to 0.0 m additional depth where the ancestor kept drilling.

  
No more blur: carving uses compact, normal-squashed surface-shell kernels with stochastic grain modulation — not wide smooth deltas — and the volume is re-distanced (Eikonal + Lipschitz clamp) continuously. Headless A/B at equal removed volume: ~3× deeper pits, ~12× more surface texture.

  
Proper sculpting: CSG carve/build/smooth/flatten brushes + a stamp library (arch, spire, butte, trench, cavern, crater, boulder, dome) with smooth-union modes — every stroke is followed by redistancing so the SDF stays valid.
