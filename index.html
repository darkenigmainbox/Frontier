ABYSSAL ENGINEERING NOTES

AN ORIGINAL OCEAN ARCHITECTURE — NOT A UE WATER CLONE

1 · WAVE FIELD WITHOUT FFT

Unreal-style oceans evaluate wave spectra with compute-shader FFT cascades. ABYSSAL deliberately forbids FFT and instead sums a choppy directional cascade (gravity swell → wind chop → capillary detail) with per-wave steepness: long bands displace the mesh while the short bands the grid cannot resolve are injected as exact analytic slopes in the fragment stage, each distance-faded to its own anti-aliasing range — evaluated identically on GPU and CPU (for particles, buoyancy and spray coupling). A 12-wave Gerstner sum runs at 200 FPS on decade-old hardware, so this path is extremely GTX-friendly while staying fully art-directable.

2 · FOAM IS 100% LAGRANGIAN PARTICLES

No depth-mask foam, no Jacobian/curvature foam, no noise-breakup foam — those are all banned here. Instead up to 40,000 CPU-integrated foam particles are spawned at advected wave crests, in a pulsing shoreline surf zone, and along the boat's wake. Each frame they are splatted top-down into a foam persistence buffer (coverage + freshness channels, with decay and diffusion). The ocean shader only samples that buffer — it cannot invent a single foam texel on its own. Wakes linger and age from bright white to grey exactly like real surf.

3 · LIVE WAVE-EQUATION MEMBRANE

A 512² shallow-water heightfield (leapfrog wave equation with absorbing boundaries and physically slowed propagation) adds true fluid interaction: boat wakes, rain impacts and click-splashes propagate as real waves and displace the ocean surface + normals near the island. This mirrors Unreal's interactive ripple layer, implemented here as raw WebGL2 ping-pong buffers.

4 · CINEMATIC WATER LIGHTING

Depth-absorbed body color over an analytic island shelf, Schlick Fresnel into an analytic sky, dual-lobe sun glitter, and crest-subsurface translucency (lighting only — never foam). ACES tone mapping, matched fog, and a time-of-day sun complete the GTA-grade look at a fraction of AAA GPU cost.
