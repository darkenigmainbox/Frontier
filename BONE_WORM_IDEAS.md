# BONE WORM ECOSYSTEM - Expanded Ideas
Favorite hazard - let's make it a whole faction.

### Lore
Not a real worm. It's a OASIS construct that glitched - a Dune sandworm that tried to load a fossil asset and got stuck as bones + scrap. Now it breeds in the desert code.

### Variants - All Stop/Slow/Destroy

**1. THE GRUBS (Slow)**
Puppy-sized bone worms, 20-30 of them. They don't destroy, they latch onto wheels and weigh you down. Like tire barnacles.
- Gameplay: Each grub = -5% speed. Shake them off by drifting hard.
- Visual: Small skulls with spine tails, chattering teeth.

**2. THE SHEDDER (Stop + Track Changer)**
Adult worm molts every lap. When it molts, it sheds giant ribs/vertebrae that STAY on track as new permanent obstacles.
- Gameplay: Track gets harder every lap. 1st lap clean, 3rd lap maze of bones.
- Visual: Worm arching, bones raining down.

**3. THE TUNNELER (Shortcut / Risk)**
A dead fossilized Titan Worm, its ribcage is a tunnel shortcut.
- Gameplay: Cut 30% of track, but inside it's dark, low grip (marrow slime), and Grubs live inside. High risk high reward.
- Visual: Driving inside glowing ribcage, light shafts.

**4. THE ARMORED SCRAP WORM (Destroy)**
Worm that has eaten 100 race cars, their chassis fused to its bones as armor. Only weak point is the skull mouth.
- Gameplay: You have to shoot its mouth to make it retreat. If you hit its armored body, YOU take damage.
- Visual: Car doors, tires, spoilers stuck to bone.

**5. THE KING - THREE-HEADED (Boss / Race Event)**
Final lap boss. Three different skulls: T-Rex, Triceratops, Human mech skull. Each head has different attack.
- T-Rex: Bite and hold (STOP)
- Triceratops: Charge and ram (DESTROY)
- Human: Scream that scrambles HUD (SLOW)
- Gameplay: All players have to dodge it at once, or one player lures it into other players.

**6. THE SAND TRAP LURE**
Bones sticking out of sand in a perfect circle, looks like loot / safe zone. It's the worm's teeth. When you enter, mouth closes.
- Gameplay: Antlion pit but with teeth that close. Need teammate to hit the gums to open.

**7. BONE BRIDGE**
After you kill a worm (shoot its brain), its corpse becomes a bridge over a canyon for 20 seconds before sinking. 
- Gameplay: Do you kill it early to make a shortcut for everyone, or kill it late to deny others?

**8. EGG FIELD**
Field of giant skull eggs. Drive slow = they don't hatch. Drive fast/honk = they hatch into Grubs.
- Audio tell: Heartbeat inside eggs.

### Mechanics to make it Ready Player One

- **Vibration Meter:** HUD has a vibration bar. Full throttle fills it. When full, worm comes. So you have to pulse throttle in open desert.
- **Bone Currency:** When worm sheds, bones can be picked up as temporary armor.
- **Lure Horn:** Legendary item - air horn made of bone that summons worm to your location, use it to drop worm on leaders.

### Combo with Desert

- Worm + Glass Sea: Worm can't burrow through glass, so Glass Sea is safe zone.
- Worm + Solar Tower: Worm is scared of intense light, beam repels it.
- Worm + Turbine Graveyard: Worm gets tangled in turbine cables.
