# T-REX JUNKYARD MECH - Bone Structure Robot
## The Apex Hazard

### Concept
A 60ft tall T-Rex built from a real fossil skeleton that scavengers rebuilt with junkyard parts. The bones ARE the chassis. Metal is just muscle and armor bolted on. It's not alive, it's a glitched OASIS guardian that thinks the race track is its nest.

Think: Jurassic Park + Mad Max + Iron Giant.

### Visual Design
- **Skeleton:** Real T-Rex bones, yellowed, cracked, wired together with steel cables
- **Muscle:** Hydraulic pistons, rusted shock absorbers, chains
- **Armor:** Car doors, hoods, stop signs, shipping containers bolted to ribs and skull
- **Head:** T-Rex skull with a car engine inside the mouth as a power core, headlights for eyes that track players, lower jaw powered by two V8 pistons
- **Tail:** Counterweight made of a whole school bus, swings to balance
- **Feet:** Crushed monster truck tires as feet pads

### How it Kills Racers - 3 Attack Modes

**1. SLOW - The Roar / Dust Stomp (Area Denial)**
- Slams foot, creates shockwave + dust cloud that covers 200ft of track
- Effect: Everyone in cloud = -50% visibility, engines choke on bone dust, mini-map disabled for 6 sec
- Tell: Raises foot, hydraulics hiss, ground cracks

**2. STOP - The Gate / Cage**
- Lies down across canyon, forms a gate. Its ribcage is the only way through, but ribs open/close like a jail.
- Effect: You have to time entry through moving ribs. Get caught = ribs clamp, you're trapped, need to shoot the piston to open.
- Gameplay: Becomes a moving door puzzle at 150mph

**3. DESTROY - The Chomp / Tail Whip**
- **Chomp:** Lunges head down and bites at lead car. If it grabs you, it lifts you 40ft and shakes, then throws you.
- **Tail Whip:** 40ft tail with bus on end sweeps across track, full wipeout - sends cars flying into canyon walls
- Counter: Its engine core glows in mouth when charging chomp - shoot the glow to stun it for 5 sec, it collapses and becomes a ramp

### Bonus Mechanics - Ready Player One Style

**A. Bone Drop Loot**
When you damage it, it drops bones that become:
- Temporary ramps
- Shields (strap a rib to your roof)
- Weapons (throw a tooth like a caltrop)

**B. Nest Behavior**
It has a nest of car wrecks it protects. If you drive near nest, it aggros. If you steal a golden tire from nest, you get huge boost but it hunts ONLY you for 30 seconds - you can lure it into other players.

**C. Multi-Stage Boss Race**
Lap 1: Sleeping, just an obstacle you drive under
Lap 2: Wakes up, starts stomping
Lap 3: Fully enraged, breaks free from chains and chases pack

**D. Weak Point - The Spine Battery**
On its back, a glowing car battery stack powers it. If a player ramps off canyon wall and lands on its back, they can rip the battery, disabling it and giving their team infinite boost for 15 sec.

### Sound Design
- Bones creaking + metal screeching
- V8 engine roar mixed with T-Rex roar
- Hydraulic hiss when it moves jaw
- When it dies: sad dinosaur whimper + engine sputter

### Track Integration
- Put it in a narrow slot canyon so you can't go around, you MUST go through/under it
- Its footprints become permanent mud traps
- After it dies, its skeleton becomes the final bridge to finish line
