# CACTUS CAMOUFLAGE CREATURES - Desert Plant Hazards

## Core Idea
In a dense cactus forest, 70% are real cactus, 30% are creatures that LOOK like cactus. You can't tell until you're too close. Paranoia hazard.

---

### 1. THE SAGUARO MIMIC - "The Grabber" (STOP)
Looks exactly like a 15ft saguaro cactus.
- **Idle:** Perfectly still, even has fake birds nesting on it.
- **Trigger:** Car passes within 10ft at >50mph - vibration triggers it.
- **Attack:** Arms snap down like a Venus flytrap, grab roof/cage, lift car 10ft and hold for 4 seconds, spines pop tires.
- **Tell:** Only tell is slightly too symmetrical, or a faint breathing movement, or no shadow.
- **Counter:** Honk before entering field - mimics flinch. Or shoot cacti to test.

### 2. BARREL CACTUS WORM - "The Prickly Train" (SLOW + DESTROY)
Long worm where each segment is a living barrel cactus.
- **Body:** 10-20 segments, each a barrel cactus with spines. Head is a cactus flower that opens into a mouth.
- **Movement:** Burrows shallow, only top of cactus segments visible - looks like a row of cacti in a line.
- **Attack:** When car drives over the line, it arches up, wrapping around car, spines puncture everything. Like a spiky constrictor.
- **Mechanic:** Each segment can be shot off, becomes a rolling spiky ball hazard.
- **Visual:** Green cactus flesh with brown dried bone inside.

### 3. CHOLLA CRAWLERS - "Jumping Cholla Worms" (SLOW)
Real cholla cactus is called "jumping cholla" because segments detach and stick to you. Make it literal.
- **Creature:** Small cactus ball with 6 tiny root-legs, moves like a crab. Hides in sand at base of real cholla.
- **Attack:** Swarm - 20-30 attach to tires, each = -3% speed, they grow and swell with air from tires. Car gets covered in spiky balls.
- **Counter:** Drive through fire / solar beam to burn them off, or drift hard to fling them onto other players.
- **Fun:** They make a squeaky toy sound when popped.

### 4. PRICKLY PEAR SLAPPER - (STOP)
Flat prickly pear cactus that is actually a camouflaged trapdoor.
- **Mechanic:** Looks like low cactus you can drive over. When you do, the pads slap up like a bear trap, clamping wheels.
- **Visual:** Pads have mouths on underside.

### 5. THE CACTUS FIELD - The Paranoia Maze (Area Hazard)
Entire section of track is a dense cactus forest 200m wide. You MUST go through.
- **70% real cactus:** Hit = -20% speed, tire damage
- **30% mimics:** Hit = grabbed/stopped
- **Gameplay:** Forces players to slow to 30mph and weave, or risk full speed and pray. Leaders get punished, back markers can catch up.
- **Tell System:** At night, real cactus flowers glow faintly under UV. Mimics don't. Give players UV headlights as risk/reward item.
- **Audio:** Real cactus silent, mimics have faint heartbeat if you get close.

### 6. OCOTILLO WHIP - "The Lasso"
Ocotillo plant has long whip-like branches. Creature version whips.
- **Attack:** Long spiny whips lash out 20ft and wrap around roll cage, yank you sideways into real cacti.
- **Counter:** Cut whips with boost exhaust.

### 7. CACTUS GOLEM - Mini Boss
Many small cacti fuse into a 20ft golem.
- **Formed when:** You shoot too many mimics, their parts combine.
- **Attack:** Rolls through field like a tumbleweed golem, smashing everything.
- **Weakness:** Water - if you have water item, it swells and explodes.

### Game Design Notes

**Camouflage Rules:**
- Never make mimic 100% undetectable. Always have 2-sec tell: slightly different color, eyes, breathing, no birds, wrong shadow.
- Use audio: faint heartbeat, creaking wood vs fleshy sound.
- Make players learn to read the desert.

**Ready Player One Twist:**
These are not natural. They are OASIS bio-engineered pets from a Poison Ivy DLC that went feral and now infest the race. You can find a "Herbicide Spray" power-up that makes all mimics reveal for 10 sec (they turn red and scream).

**Combos:**
- Cactus Worm + Bone Worm = cactus worm lives inside bone worm ribcage
- Cactus Field + Solar Tower = solar beam burns field, reveals mimics by making them wilt
- Cholla Crawlers + Glass Sea = crawlers can't grip glass, safe zone
