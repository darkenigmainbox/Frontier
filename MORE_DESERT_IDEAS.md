# MORE DESERT HAZARDS - Batch 3
Fresh, no repeats.

### ROCK / GEOLOGICAL

**1. Mesa Golem - Camouflage Mountain (STOP/DESTROY)**
A mesa IS the creature. Sleeps for 1000 years, looks like normal red rock.
- Trigger: Too many cars honk / shoot near it, or race on its back.
- Attack: Stands up, 80ft tall, made of layered rock plates. Throws boulders, steps on track blocking it. Its footprints become new pits.
- Tell: Perfectly flat top, birds avoid it, has two dark cave eyes.
- Counter: Don't wake it. If awake, drive between its legs.

**2. Petrified Forest Domino (STOP)**
Forest of stone trees, dead silent. Wind or car vibration topples one.
- Effect: Chain reaction - 20 trees fall like dominoes across track in sequence. You have to time slalom through falling 30ft stone trunks.
- Gameplay: First player triggers it, everyone behind suffers. Risk/reward to be first or last.

**3. Salt Crystal Bloom (STOP - Prison)**
White salt flat that looks safe. It's supersaturated.
- Effect: Vibration causes instant crystal growth - giant 10ft salt crystals sprout in 2 seconds around your car, caging you. Like ice prison.
- Tell: Ground crackles, faint chiming sound.
- Counter: Nitro shatters crystals, but shards become caltrops for others.

**4. Fossil Coral Reef - The Shredder (SLOW/DESTROY)**
Ancient ocean reef now desert. White, razor-sharp coral 3ft tall covering 100m.
- Effect: Not tall enough to stop, but drives over = tires shredded, undercarriage scraped. Sparks. Leaves you on rims.
- Visual: Beautiful white reef, looks like snow.

### SAND / FAUNA

**5. Sand Shark - Land Shark (DESTROY)**
Not a worm, a shark. Fin cuts sand like water, leaves perfect wake.
- Effect: Hunts fastest car. Circles, then breaches and bites, flipping car. You see fin only.
- Tell: Sand wake, JAWS music sting.
- Counter: Drive over rocky section - it can't swim through rock, gets beached.

**6. Scorpion Emperor Pit (STOP)**
Giant scorpion nest under thin sand crust.
- Effect: Crust breaks, you fall into pit with 3 giant scorpions. Tails sting and flip cars, pincers grab and hold.
- Counter: Scorpions fight each other if you throw a flare.

**7. Dust Devil Prison (SLOW)**
A dust devil that doesn't move. If you drive into it, you get stuck in eye.
- Effect: You're spinning slowly inside, can't see out, controls reversed. Need to accelerate out through wall.
- Visual: Inside is calm, outside is wall of sand.

### MAN-MADE / WASTELAND TECH

**8. Oil Derrick Fire Geysers (DESTROY)**
Field of abandoned oil pumps that still have pressure, but now pump fire.
- Effect: Timed fire geysers shoot 30ft across track. Direct hit = instant engine fire. Leaves burning oil puddle.
- Tell: Creaking pump, whistle before blast.
- Gameplay: Use shadow of pump to see timing.

**9. Billboard Guillotine (STOP)**
Old Route 66 billboards, rusted, hanging by one cable.
- Effect: Wind or sonic boom makes them fall flat across track like a gate. Creates instant wall.
- Visual: Funny ads like "SEE THE THING!" still visible.

**10. Hourglass Canyon (SLOW - Track Changer)**
Giant hourglass built into canyon, 100ft tall, filled with sand.
- Effect: Every 60 seconds it flips, pouring a river of sand that blocks left or right lane. Track changes each lap.
- Gameplay: You have to memorize flip timer.

### WEIRD / OASIS GLITCH

**11. Mercury River - Quicksilver (SLOW/DESTROY)**
River of liquid mercury, mirror finish.
- Effect: Looks solid like Glass Sea, but it's liquid metal. Ultra slippery + toxic - corrodes tires. Reflects solar tower beam randomly.
- Visual: Perfect mirror, you see sky in ground.

**12. Gravity Meteor (SLOW - Chaos)**
Crashed meteorite creates low-gravity bubble 100ft wide.
- Effect: Inside bubble, cars float, lose grip, tumble slowly. You can jump over obstacles but can't steer.
- Tell: Rocks floating around it, dust hangs in air.

**13. Mirage Oasis Siren (STOP - Lure)**
Beautiful oasis with water and palm trees appears off track.
- Effect: If you swerve to it (looks like shortcut with water to cool engine), it's quicksand + mud that sucks you down. The palm trees are actually Cactus Mimics.
- Tell: Too green, no tire tracks leading to it.

**14. Vulture Scrap Drop (DESTROY - Air)**
Giant mechanical vultures nesting on buttes.
- Effect: They pick up car wrecks and drop them on racers below like bombs.
- Counter: Honk to scare them, they drop early.

**15. Tar Snake - Living Road (SLOW)**
A river of tar that moves like a snake across desert.
- Effect: Slithers across track, tries to wrap around tires. Leaves sticky trail for 30 sec.
- Visual: Shiny black, eyes are oil bubbles.
